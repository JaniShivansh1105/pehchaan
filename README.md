# PEHCHAAN

**AI-Powered Financial Intelligence for the Underserved**

> No traditional credit history should not mean no financial identity.

---

## What Is PEHCHAAN?

PEHCHAAN is an integrated FinTech prototype that transforms overlooked everyday financial behaviour into understandable financial intelligence. It is designed for financially underserved and credit-invisible users -- people who participate in the economy every day but remain invisible to formal credit and investment systems.

## The Problem

Millions of people have no traditional credit history -- no loans, no credit cards, no bureau records. Yet they recharge their phones consistently, pay utility bills, make online purchases, and transact digitally every day. These everyday behaviours carry meaningful financial signals that traditional credit systems ignore entirely.

At the same time, first-time investors with small amounts (INR 500 - INR 5,000/month) lack accessible, jargon-free guidance to take their first confident step into investment.

## The Solution

PEHCHAAN addresses both challenges as one connected financial journey:

### UNDERSTAND -> IMPROVE -> GROW

| Phase | What It Does |
|-------|-------------|
| **UNDERSTAND** | Analyses alternative financial signals (recharge patterns, utility payments, e-commerce activity) to produce an interpretable Credit Readiness Score with plain-language explanations |
| **IMPROVE** | Provides prioritised improvement recommendations and a What-If Simulator where users can experiment with behavioural changes and see their estimated effect |
| **GROW** | Conducts a conversational risk assessment, provides educational micro-investment guidance, and visualises simulated growth scenarios for small monthly contributions |

## Core Modules

1. **Financial Behaviour Profile** -- Input or simulate alternative financial signals
2. **Credit Readiness Intelligence** -- Score, risk classification, top-3 factors
3. **Explainability** -- Plain-language explanation of contributing factors
4. **Improvement Pathway** -- Prioritised actionable recommendations
5. **What-If Financial Simulator** -- Modify variables, compare current vs. simulated readiness
6. **Investment Risk Assessment** -- 7-question conversational risk profiling
7. **Educational Investment Guidance** -- Allocation across broad instrument categories
8. **Growth Simulator** -- 3-scenario simulated growth visualisation (1-5 years)

## Responsible Use Disclaimer

PEHCHAAN is a hackathon prototype for educational and demonstration purposes.

- The **Credit Readiness Score** is a model-generated indicator based on alternative behavioural signals. It is **not** an official credit bureau score and does not affect real credit applications.
- All **investment guidance** is educational. It does not constitute regulated financial or investment advice. Users should consult a registered financial advisor before making investment decisions.
- The prototype uses **synthetic data**. No real financial data is collected or processed.
- **Growth projections** are simulated using assumed return rates. Actual investment returns vary and are subject to market risk.
